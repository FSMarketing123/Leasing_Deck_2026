Marketing & Technology

Our Platforms.

A modern marketing stack — built around data, video, immersive media, and AI — designed to position assets, reach the right tenants, and accelerate decisions.

01 · Digital Brochures

Digital, Data-Driven Brochures.

We leverage interactive digital brochures to showcase property highlights, current availabilities, and key market insights. Each brochure is enriched with Placer.ai data — providing foot traffic analytics, visitor demographics, and trade area patterns — offering prospects a clear, data-backed view of the opportunity.

02 · Market Videos

Market Videos with Aerial Insights.

Our custom market overview videos combine high-quality drone footage with detailed analysis of the surrounding retail landscape. These videos highlight competitive centers, traffic counts, access points, and key co-tenancies — giving prospects a clear, visual understanding of the property's position within the market and greatly reducing the decision timeline.

03 · VR Tours

Virtual Walkthroughs.

We create our own VR walkthroughs for each vacancy, giving prospects a detailed, immersive look at the space from anywhere. This allows tenants to evaluate the layout early in the process — without needing to travel — saving time and reducing the risk of unproductive site visits. As a result, we increase virtual tour volume and help tenants make faster, more informed decisions.

04 · AI Integration

AI Prospecting with Smart Follow-Up.

We use AI to scale our tenant outreach and automate follow-ups, allowing us to focus on high-level strategy and deal negotiations. Our system connects with a curated retail database that continually refines tenant contact info and expansion territories. This ensures we're targeting the right decision-makers in the right markets — maximizing efficiency and improving the quality of every touchpoint.

05 · Social Media

Social Media Presence.

We create targeted social media campaigns to showcase property highlights and availabilities, reaching the right tenant audiences with precision. Each campaign is optimized with platform analytics, ensuring maximum visibility, engagement, and lead generation for your property.

Leasing Strategy

Our Process.

Data

Data-Driven Targeting & Strategy

We build out tenant target lists using in-depth demographic data, market growth trends, and retail void analysis. This approach allows us to identify which retailers are best suited for the trade area — ensuring outreach efforts are focused, strategic, and aligned with both market demand and tenant expansion patterns. 

Trade Area AnalysisDemographics, traffic patterns, and consumer spending data mapped to your asset.
Competitive Set ReviewMapping vacancy, rent trends, and tenant mix across competing centers.
Void AnalysisIdentifying whitespace opportunities and highest-demand retail categories for your submarket.
Prospects

Tenant Prospecting & Outreach

Leveraging our proprietary tenant database and national relationships to target the right retailers — before they're in the public market. We reach decision-makers directly, not through intermediaries.

Direct Retailer AccessRelationships with national, regional, and local retailers across all categories.
Proprietary DatabaseTenant expansion targets, deal velocity, and contact intelligence unavailable on public platforms.
Targeted CampaignCustom marketing packages, site plans, and outreach materials that drive qualified interest.
Strategy

Bi-Weekly Reporting For Real-Time Strategy

We provide detailed bi-weekly reports outlining all communication with prospects and tenants in negotiation. These updates give our clients actionable insights to share with their teams, helping guide strategic decisions. By consistently delivering real-time feedback, we stay aligned with our clients and quickly adapt to market trends as they emerge. 

Consistent CommunicationDetailed bi-weekly reporting on prospect outreach, leasing activity, and active negotiations.
Actionable Market Intelligence Real-time feedback on tenant interest, objections, market trends, and competitive activity to support informed decision-making.
Strategic Alignment Ongoing collaboration with ownership and asset management teams to refine leasing strategy and quickly adapt to changing market conditions.
